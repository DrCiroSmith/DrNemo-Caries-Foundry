Clinical Pathology & Why GtfB Matters

In clinical dentistry, dental caries is driven by acidogenic biofilms. Streptococcus mutans secretes Glucosyltransferase-B (GtfB), an exoenzyme that cleaves dietary sucrose and synthesizes sticky, water-insoluble α-1,3-linked glucans (mutan). This matrix forms an impenetrable bacterial fortress on tooth enamel.
