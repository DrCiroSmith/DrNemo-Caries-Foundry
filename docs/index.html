Clinical Pathology & Why GtfB Matters

In clinical dentistry, dental caries is driven by acidogenic biofilms. Streptococcus mutans secretes Glucosyltransferase-B (GtfB), an exoenzyme that cleaves dietary sucrose and synthesizes sticky, water-insoluble α-1,3-linked glucans (mutan). This matrix forms an impenetrable bacterial fortress on tooth enamel.

The Engineering Solution

Rather than broadly killing oral flora with antiseptics, Dr. Nemo attacks the enzymatic architecture. We engineered a closed-loop multi-agent directed evolution platform in LangGraph:

  • CycleDesigner Node: Generates conformationally constrained cyclic disulfide peptides (C...C).
  • Chitosan Shield: Excludes I, K, D, E to prevent entrapment within topical mucoadhesive chitosan nanoparticles.
  • OpenFold3 & DiffDock: Resolves atomic interactions against the GtfB catalytic triad (Asp477, Glu515, Asp588).
  • IGSC Biosecurity Gate: Confirms sequence safety prior to solid-phase synthesis formatting.
